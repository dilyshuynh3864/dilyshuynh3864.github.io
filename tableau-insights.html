Dashboard 01: Global Sales Distribution & Customer Segments

Business Context: Designed for sales operations leads to track revenue distribution by nation, monitor customer transactional volumes, and identify structural ticket size distribution bounds.

Open Dashboard on Tableau Public

Core Insight Discovered

The geographic tree-map isolates the **USA** as the primary driver of absolute revenue blocks, followed by France. Concurrently, the sales distribution histogram exposes a heavy right-skewed distribution, proving that the vast majority of transactions concentrate strictly around the $2,000 to $4,000 threshold, with a sharp drop-off in high-ticket values.

Strategic Outcome & Action

Enables supply chain and logistics managers to optimize regional inventory safety stocks inside top revenue centers. Additionally, marketing divisions can construct specific cross-selling bundles targeted at the $3,000 buyer segment to increase average order values.

Dashboard 02: Product Line Performance & Order Status Mapping

Business Context: Targeted at product category managers and fulfillment operations teams to discover revenue variances across asset classes, deal configurations, and chronological monthly spikes.

Open Dashboard on Tableau Public

Core Insight Discovered

The product line bubble chart reveals that Classic Cars holds the largest market share volume, with a distinct seasonal surge peaking sharply in November (breaking over $1.02M in sales). Furthermore, the pipeline tracking metrics show that an overwhelming majority of total deals are successfully finalized as 'Shipped', while 'Medium' sized contracts dominate overall deal profiles.

Strategic Outcome & Action

Provides clear procurement foresight, confirming that operational capacity and advertising budgets must be dynamically scaled up starting in Q3 to handle the predictable November peak. It also supports optimizing production lines to prioritize medium-deal assets.

Dashboard 03: COVID-19 Global Infection & Mortality Dynamics

Business Context: Tailored for healthcare policymakers to monitor global infectious distributions, locate regional mortality nodes, and audit historical longitudinal trends.

Open Dashboard on Tableau Public

Core Insight Discovered

Based on a global pool of over 150M documented cases, the geospatial mapping layer marks the **United States** and **Europe** as the heaviest historical transmission zones, with Europe bearing the highest total death count burden. The predictive timeline visualizes a sustained, exponential infection escalation beginning late 2020, with the US forecasting a steep trajectory up toward a 19.11% population infection density point by late 2021.

Strategic Outcome & Action

Supplies public health bodies with actionable, localized risk matrices to pre-allocate medical resources, establish targeted border monitoring protocols, and measure the long-term effectiveness of non-pharmaceutical containment strategies.
