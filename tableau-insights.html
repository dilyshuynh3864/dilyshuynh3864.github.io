Tableau Dashboards:
Insights & Outcomes

A technical showcase of converting operational log metrics and demographic distributions into interactive, decision-ready analytical interfaces.

Technical Analytics Stack

Tableau Public/Desktop Calculated Fields & Parameters Level of Detail (LOD) Expressions Interactive Data Storytelling Geospatial Mapping Set & Filter Actions

Analytics Methodology

Each dashboard was engineered using a three-stage pipeline to ensure data fidelity and executive-level clarity:

Methodology Phase Technical Implementation
Data Integration Cleaned raw operational logs via SQL/Python before ingestion to ensure standardized schema.
Metric Engineering Developed custom Calculated Fields and LOD Expressions to surface key performance indicators (KPIs) like order-to-ship ratios.
UI/UX Design Utilized interactivity (Set/Filter Actions) to allow stakeholders to perform self-service exploration of localized datasets.

Featured Dashboards

Dashboard 01: Global Sales Distribution

Core Insight

Revenue is highly concentrated in the USA and France. Transaction volume distribution is right-skewed, peaking at the $2k–$4k range.

Strategic Action

Optimized inventory safety stocks for high-revenue regions and designed cross-selling bundles for the $3k buyer segment to boost AOV.

Dashboard 02: Product Performance

Core Insight

Classic Cars lead volume share, with a repeatable seasonal spike in November (>$1M revenue). 'Shipped' status dominates the fulfillment pipeline.

Strategic Action

Scaled advertising and supply chain capacity starting in Q3 to prepare for the predictable November surge.

Dashboard 03: COVID-19 Global Dynamics

Core Insight

USA/Europe identified as high-transmission epicenters. Predictive modeling showed a 19.11% population infection density trajectory for the US by late 2021.

Strategic Action

Provided public health bodies with actionable risk matrices for medical resource allocation and border control protocols.
