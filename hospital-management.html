Problem Statement & Solutions

Modern clinical prediction tools risk mirroring historical systemic disparities if deployed without governance. Furthermore, operational class imbalances cause traditional machine learning classifiers to under-detect high-risk patient readmissions, while administrative teams lack clear visibility into how complex polypharmacy drug combinations drive hospital Length of Stay (LOS) capacity drops.

To solve this, our team developed a dual-pipeline engine: a Clinical Fairness Layer that utilizes blind model training and cost-sensitive calibration to minimize systemic subgroup variance, and a Resource Optimization Layer that transforms chemical distribution paths into transactional datasets to map hospital resource consumption metrics.

System Architecture & Engineering

1. Cost-Sensitive XGBoost & Fairlearn Auditing

Instead of relying on standard balanced frameworks, incoming features are cast directly into categorical indices. Models are trained blindly without access to sensitive group labels. The pipeline monitors predictive balance by evaluating outputs across moving penalty weight factors to find an optimal equilibrium point:

  • Disparity Compression: Safely brought the False Negative Rate gap ($\Delta FNR$) down under the 10% international standard line.
  • Staff Alert Management: Maintained an operational selection rate rolling between 30.6% and 36.7% to shield clinical personnel from predictive alert fatigue.
  • Discharge Safeguard: Calibrated decision boundaries to flag at-risk readmissions before resource allocation occurs.

2. Co-Prescription Market Basket & Inferential Modeling

To isolate how drug combinations impact physical bed availability, active medication records were processed as distinct clinical transaction vectors. The system mines frequent itemsets across a 1% support baseline and checks relationship ties using strict association filters (Lift $\ge 1.2$):

Transaction Encoder
(Step 1)

Transforms raw multi-row prescription records into a sparse boolean matrix, isolating active changes ('Steady', 'Up', 'Down') across 23 target diabetes compounds.

Apriori Processing
(Step 2)

Extracts frequent itemsets and maps co-occurrence laws to bypass minor clinical noise and focus purely on highly repetitive treatment phacodos.

Parametric Pairwise Suite
(Step 3)

Runs a global One-Way ANOVA followed by Post-Hoc Tukey HSD tests to control family-wise error rates and flag pairs that cause distinct hospital stay extensions.

3. Production Integration & EHR Asset Packaging

When a patient enters a discharge loop, the host application channels their real-time clinical feature vector directly to the serialized model runtime. The engine uses packaged artifacts (final_model.pkl, label_encoder.pkl, and cat_mapping.pkl) to execute rapid inference, ensuring instant operational response times without requiring external runtime recompilations.

System Limitations & Future Roadmap

Demonstrating modern engineering maturity requires identifying current pipeline boundaries and detailing actionable infrastructure optimization paths:

  • Current Architectural Limits: The platform processes demographic bias checks post-hoc rather than integrating real-time in-flight regularizers, and statistical validations depend on cold, historical dataset updates.
  • Future Optimization Roadmap: Integrate Fairlearn grid-search reduction algorithms directly into the active training loop, build dynamic data hooks for real-time streaming updates, and expand the market basket engine to monitor broader comorbidity interaction rules.
