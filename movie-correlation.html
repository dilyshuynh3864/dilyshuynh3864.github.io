Movie Correlation Analysis
Multi-Variable Statistical Mapping

Utilizing the Python scientific computing stack to determine key operational factors affecting entertainment industry revenue trends.

The Data Problem

Identifying revenue-driving attributes in the film industry requires robust mathematical mapping. The focus of this research was to discover whether budget size, public user ratings, or specific production companies hold the highest mathematical correlation with total worldwide gross revenue outputs.

Technical Actions & Exploratory Insights

  • Data Ingestion & Cleaning (Pandas): Audited the data profile to drop incomplete records, cast distorted floating-point columns into integer indicators, and parsed messy text cells to extract production years.
  • Outlier & Distribution Analysis: Constructed automated scatter plots using Matplotlib and Seaborn to analyze the interaction patterns between production budget sizes and revenue parameters.
  • Statistical Matrix Mapping: Executed comprehensive Pearson, Spearman, and Kendall correlation matrices to extract precise multi-variable linear trend directions.
  • Categorical Factorization: Transformed qualitative parameters (e.g., Company Names, Directors, Genres) into numeric categorical indices to map all features into a unified correlation visualization heatmap.
